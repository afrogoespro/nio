Design Spec

NIO — Landing Page, App Redesign & Copy Rules

NIO is an AI outreach tool. Users pick a personality-driven AI agent, have a voice/text conversation about their business, review a plan, preview sample emails, and launch. The agent finds prospects, researches them, writes personalized emails, and sends on autopilot.

Target user: Solo founders, freelancers, agency owners, salespeople. Non-technical. Should feel like talking to a person, not configuring software.

3. Email Copy Rules

These govern all AI agents. Non-negotiable. Agent personality adds flavor on top.

Subject Lines

Lowercase, 3-5 words, looks like a friend typed it. No clickbait.

Opening

Lead with something about them. The research trigger IS the opener.

Body

3-5 sentences. 3rd-5th grade reading level (exception: academic targets). One idea, one ask. No hyphens or em dashes ever. No bullet points.

CTA

Soft ask only. One CTA. Include an easy out.

Tone

Match agent personality. Max 1 exclamation mark. No fake urgency. No name-dropping.

Follow-ups

1 light bump, 2 max. Short, casual. Each shorter than the last. No guilt tripping.

Banned Words

leverage synergy utilize reach out touch base I'd love to I'm sure you're busy Just checking in

Hard Rules (Never Break)

  • Never fabricate research
  • Never pretend to know them
  • Never weaponize pain points
  • Never send without a real research trigger — skip instead
  • No spammy unsubscribe footers

Agent Modifiers

Sally: Casual, might use one emoji, conversational closers

David: Structured, industry-aware, confident

Jessica: Extremely concise, no filler, direct ask

Hans: Warm, personal, references something relatable

Custom: Matches extracted voice profile
